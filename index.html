Arrive & settle in

A gentle first evening in the capital.

Arrival
Afternoon

Check in & unwind

Arrive in Gangtok, settle into the hotel and rest after the mountain transfer.

Late afternoon

MG Marg

Explore the pedestrian promenade, browse the shops and stop for hot drinks.

Evening

Dinner in town

Keep dinner close to MG Marg and return early.

Quick fact
The setting

MG Marg is Gangtok’s vehicle-free town-centre promenade, made for strolling rather than traffic.

Fun fact
A Sikkim first

In 2016, Sikkim became India’s first fully organic state. Look out for local produce and traditional snacks along the walk.

Keep the first evening flexible if the journey takes longer than planned.

Bakthang Waterfall

Quick fact
The waterfall

Ban Jhakri Falls drops roughly 30 metres. Its park combines gardens, walkways and displays of traditional healers.

Fun fact
Behind the name

In local folklore, “ban jhakri” means forest shaman. The sculptures tell stories of the healers associated with the hills.

Ask the driver to confirm the most efficient local sequence on the day.

The high-altitude lake

Tsomgo Lake, subject to permit and road clearance.

Weather dependent
07:30

Depart Gangtok

Leave early with the arranged vehicle and required permits.

Late morning

Tsomgo (Changu) Lake

Enjoy the lake and mountain scenery. Snow cover and activity availability depend on conditions.

Early afternoon

Return journey

Allow generous time for changing road and weather conditions.

Late afternoon

Rest in Gangtok

Keep the evening free after the altitude and travel.

Quick fact
Mountain facts

Tsomgo sits around 3,750 metres above sea level and is fed by snowmelt from the surrounding mountains.

Local legend
A lake with legends

Local tradition says Buddhist monks once read changes in the lake’s colour to predict events. This is folklore, not a forecast.

If the road is closed, use the day for local Gangtok sights and cafés. At approximately 3,750 m, do not ignore headache, nausea or dizziness.

The monastery

Enchey grew from the hermitage of Drubthob Karpo; its name is associated with a secluded or solitary place.

Good to know
Look closely

The monastery’s architecture blends striking rooflines and decorative detail. The ropeway gives a completely different view of the city.

Ropeway opening and queues can vary during the holiday period.

Across the hills to Pelling

One scenic transfer, with an optional stop en route.

Hotel change
08:30

Check out & depart

Start after breakfast with luggage ready for the full transfer.

En route

Ravangla, if practical

A brief Buddha Park stop is possible if the driver uses this route and conditions allow.

Mid / late afternoon

Pelling hotel check-in

Allow approximately 5–6 hours of road time, plus any stops.

Evening

Relax at the hotel

Keep the evening open; mountain views depend on visibility.

Quick fact
The mountain

Kangchenjunga rises to 8,586 metres, the world’s third-highest summit. Clear mornings give the best chance of seeing it.

Fun fact
More than a mountain

The Kangchenjunga landscape is sacred in local traditions; Khangchendzonga National Park became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2016.

Confirm the road via Ravangla with the driver rather than planning a compulsory detour.

Return to Pelling

Quick fact
Sacred waters

Khecheopalri Lake, about 34 km from Pelling, is revered by Buddhists and Hindus. Swimming and boating are not permitted.

Local legend
The leaf legend

Local legend says birds remove fallen leaves from the lake to keep its surface clean. Enjoy the story, and leave the lake undisturbed.

Local road conditions may change the stop order; avoid adding further distant sights.

Royal history

Rabdentse served as the second capital of the former Kingdom of Sikkim from 1670 to 1814.

Fun fact
Up at the Skywalk

The glass-bottomed Skywalk stands near a 137-foot statue of Chenrezig, the bodhisattva of compassion.

Rabdentse involves a walk; skip it if the children are tired or the path is slippery.

Along the way

Before you go

Travel notes

A few checks that matter during a late-December trip.

01 /Tsomgo permits

Arrange the required protected-area permit through an authorised operator. Confirm permit status and road access the day before. Permit information ↗

02 /Winter conditions

Carry warm layers and suitable shoes. Snow, fog or road restrictions may change the high-altitude excursion; avoid pushing ahead in poor conditions.

03 /Transfers & departure

Pre-arrange both hotel transfers. Leave a substantial buffer before the onward train; road conditions can change quickly.
